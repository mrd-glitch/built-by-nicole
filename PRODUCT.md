## Purpose and users
Built by Nicole provides personal fitness and nutrition coaching for women with full lives, including busy moms. The public page serves mostly social and referral visitors who want to understand Nicole's approach before applying. Nicole uses the admin portal; approved clients use the client portal.

## Confirmed workflow
An applicant completes the existing intake. Nicole reviews it and follows up to assess fit. Approved clients receive an invitation and personal plans, weekly check-ins, and messaging. Payments and consultation booking are future work.
